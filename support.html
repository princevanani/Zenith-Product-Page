Get in touch

Found a form Zenith doesn't fill correctly, a field it should recognize but doesn't, or something else that's not working right? We'd like to hear about it.

Email support support@decodebits.com

Before you write in, quick things to check

  • Auto-save is on: click the Zenith icon and check the "Auto-save fields as I type" toggle.
  • You've filled the field at least once elsewhere — Zenith learns from what you type, it doesn't come pre-loaded with your information.
  • For dropdowns built with a custom widget (not a plain native dropdown), autofill is best-effort and can occasionally miss — let us know which site, and we'll take a look.

Your data

Everything Zenith saves lives only on your device — see the Privacy Policy for details, or open Zenith's options page any time to view, edit, export, or delete your saved information.
